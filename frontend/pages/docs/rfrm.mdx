## Buyback & Burn Mechanism

### How It Works

**5% of all market yield** is used to buy back and permanently burn RFRM tokens:

```
Market Yield Flow:
100 SOL generated in yield
├─ 20 SOL → Platform (20%)
├─ 30 SOL → Gookie (30%, swapped to RFRM)
├─ 45 SOL → Users (45%)
└─ 5 SOL → Buyback & Burn (5%, creates deflationary pressure)
```

### Buyback Process

1. **Accumulation**: Platform collects 5% of yield in SOL
2. **Swap**: SOL → RFRM via Jupiter aggregator
3. **Burn**: RFRM sent to null address (`11111111111111111111111111111111`)
4. **Transparency**: All burns recorded on-chain

### Deflationary Impact

**Example calculation:**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Monthly market yield | 1,000 SOL |
| 5% allocated to buyback | 50 SOL |
| RFRM price | $0.10 |
| SOL price | $150 |
| RFRM bought & burned | 75,000 tokens/month |
| **Annual burn rate** | **900,000 tokens/year** |

At this rate, **~0.09% of supply** burned annually. As platform grows, burn rate accelerates.

## Token Mechanics

### Smart Contract Integration

**RFRM is integrated with three contracts:**

| Contract | Purpose | RFRM Use |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Gookie Auction** | NFT bidding | Lock for bids |
| **Market Escrow** | Yield distribution | Receive as yield |
| **Learning Subscription** | Content access | Lock for subscription |

### Token Locking

**How locking works:**
```
1. User approves token transfer
2. Tokens moved to program-owned account
3. Tokens marked as "locked" in state
4. User retains ownership, cannot transfer
5. Unlock when condition met (auction end, subscription cancel)
6. Tokens returned to user wallet
```

**Locking prevents:**
- ❌ Selling while locked
- ❌ Transferring to another wallet
- ❌ Using for other purposes

**Locking allows:**
- ✅ Viewing balance in wallet
- ✅ Receiving additional RFRM
- ✅ Canceling auction bid (with penalty)

## FAQ

### Is RFRM a security?

No. RFRM is a utility token required for platform functionality. It's not marketed as an investment, and there are no promises of profit.

### Can I stake RFRM?

Not yet. Staking is planned for Phase 3 (Q3 2026).

### What if pump.fun shuts down?

RFRM is an SPL token on Solana mainnet. It exists independently of pump.fun and can be traded on any Solana DEX (Raydium, Jupiter, Orca, etc.).

### Why not use SOL for everything?

SOL is used for market signals because it's neutral and widely held. RFRM creates additional utility, aligns incentives, and allows for deflationary mechanics that benefit long-term holders.

### Can the team mint more RFRM?

No. Total supply is capped at 1 billion. Mint authority has been revoked.
