## What is a Prescription Market?

Traditional prediction markets ask users to *bet* on outcomes. Referandium flips this paradigm.

Instead of passively predicting, users **prescribe the future** by signaling YES or NO with SOL. These signals don't just reflect sentiment—they influence reality through community-driven consensus and market-making dynamics.

**Core Philosophy:**
- ❌ No gambling or betting
- ✅ **Signaling** future outcomes with skin in the game
- ✅ **Prescription-based governance** where users write the future, not guess it
- ✅ Transparent, on-chain signal aggregation on Solana

## How It Works

### 1. Create a Market

Anyone can create a market on any topic — politics, crypto, sports, culture, or anything else. Define a clear question, set resolution criteria, and choose an end date. Markets go live instantly.

### 2. Signal YES or NO

Deposit SOL to signal your conviction. Your principal is always returned — you never lose your deposit. 1 wallet = 1 signal per market.

### 3. Earn Yield

Your SOL earns yield while locked in the market escrow. When the market closes, claim your full deposit plus your proportional share of the yield earned.

> 💡 **Key**: This is not betting. Your SOL is returned 100%. Yield is earned on top as a reward for participation.

## 🪙 Cause Tokens

### What are Cause Tokens?

When creating a market, creators can optionally launch a **Cause Token** — a bonding curve token powered by [Meteora](https://meteora.ag). This allows early supporters of a market's thesis to buy into the cause before it graduates to a full liquidity pool.

### How It Works

1. **Creator enables** Cause Token during market creation
2. **Bonding curve launches** on Meteora — early buyers get tokens at lower prices
3. **As demand grows**, the token price increases along the curve
4. **Graduation** — when the curve fills, the token graduates to a full AMM liquidity pool
5. **LP fees** are split between the platform and the market creator

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer**: Cause Tokens are speculative assets. They are not financial advice and carry risk of loss.

Learn more: [Cause Token](/docs/cause-token)
