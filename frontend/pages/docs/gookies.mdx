# Gookies: Verified Creators

## What is a Gookie?

A **Gookie** is a verified market creator on Referandium. Gookies are trusted curators who have been manually approved by the platform and are identified by a blue ✓ checkmark badge on their markets.

> 🍪 **Etymology**: Gookie = "Goo" (crypto slang) + "Bookie" (market operator)

### Key Characteristics

- ✓ **Verified badge** displayed on all markets they create
- 💸 **Earn 30% of yield** generated from their markets
- 🎯 **Curated quality** — only approved creators get verified status
- 🤝 **Accountable** — verified creators maintain high standards

---

## How to Become a Verified Creator

Verified status is granted by the Referandium team through a manual review process.

### Application Process

1. **Apply** — Contact the team via Twitter (@ReferandiumIO) or Discord
2. **Provide info** — Share your wallet address, community presence, and market ideas
3. **Review** — The team evaluates your track record and proposed markets
4. **Approval** — If accepted, your wallet is marked as verified
5. **Create** — Start creating markets with the ✓ badge and earning yield

### What We Look For

- Active presence in crypto/policy/sports communities
- Clear, well-defined market ideas
- History of trustworthy behavior
- Ability to attract participants and promote markets

> 💡 **Tip**: Start by creating markets as a regular user. If your markets attract good participation and you maintain quality, you're a strong candidate for verification.

## Verified Creator Responsibilities

### 1. Create Quality Markets

- Provide helpful descriptions

- Share on Twitter/X, Discord, Telegram

### 3. Act in Good Faith
- Cooperate with admin during resolution
- Report outcomes truthfully

### 4. Maintain Trust
- Consistent quality across all markets
- Transparent communication
- Build a reputation signal providers can rely on

> ⚠️ **Warning**: Verified status can be revoked for misconduct, misleading markets, or failure to maintain standards.

## Earnings: 30% Yield Share

### How Creators Earn

Markets generate yield while SOL is locked in escrow. Verified creators earn **30% of total yield** from every market they create.

### Yield Distribution

| Stakeholder | Share |
|-------------|-------|
| Platform | 20% |
| **Gookie (Creator)** | **30%** |
| Signal Providers | 45% |
| Buyback & Burn | 5% |

### Earnings Examples

| Total SOL Locked | Duration | Est. Yield | Creator's 30% |
|------------------|----------|------------|----------------|
| 50 SOL | 30 days | ~0.2 SOL | ~0.06 SOL |
| 200 SOL | 30 days | ~0.8 SOL | ~0.24 SOL |
| 1,000 SOL | 30 days | ~4.0 SOL | ~1.20 SOL |
| 5,000 SOL | 30 days | ~20.0 SOL | ~6.00 SOL |

### Factors That Increase Earnings

- ✅ More participants → more SOL locked → more yield
- ✅ Longer market duration → more yield accumulation
- ✅ Better promotion → more visibility → more signals
- ✅ Clear resolution criteria → more trust → more participation

> 💰 **The incentive is simple**: Create great markets, promote them well, and earn yield. The better your markets perform, the more you earn.

## FAQ

### How long does verification take?

Typically 1-3 days after applying. The team reviews applications in batches.

### Can I lose my verified status?

Yes. Verified status can be revoked for creating misleading markets, manipulating outcomes, abandoning markets, or other misconduct.

### Do I need RFRM tokens to become verified?

No. Verification is based on your track record and community presence, not token holdings.

### Can I create markets without verification?

Yes. Anyone can create markets. Verification gives you the ✓ badge, higher visibility, and the 30% yield share.

### How many markets can I create?

There's no limit. Create as many markets as you want across any category.
