# Gookies: Market Manager NFTs

## What is a Gookie?

A **Gookie** is an NFT that grants temporary **market management rights** on Referandium. Think of it as a hybrid between a traditional bookie (market operator) and a Web3 NFT—but with a critical twist: **Gookies aren't sold, they're rented.**

> 🍪 **Etymology**: Gookie = "Goo" (crypto slang) + "Bookie" (market operator)

### Key Characteristics

- 🎨 **Unique NFT artwork** tied to specific market themes
- ⏱️ **Time-limited rental period** (typically 30-90 days)
- 💰 **Bid with RFRM tokens**, not SOL
- 🔒 **RFRM tokens are locked** during auction period
- 🏆 **Winner gets NFT** and management rights
- 💸 **Earn 30% of yield** from your market (paid in RFRM)

## How Gookie Auctions Work

### Phase 1: Auction Launch

Platform releases themed Gookies for upcoming markets:

```
Example Gookies:
- "Fenerbahçe 2026 Transfers" → Soccer market
- "Bitcoin $100K by Q4" → Crypto market  
- "US Elections 2026" → Politics market
```

**Auction Mechanics:**
- Duration: 3-7 days
- Bid currency: **RFRM tokens only**
- Bid increments: Minimum 5% above current highest
- RFRM locked: Tokens locked until auction ends
- Auction extension: +10 minutes if bid in last 2 minutes

### Phase 2: Bidding

Users bid RFRM tokens to win management rights:

```
Bidding Flow:
1. Connect wallet with RFRM balance
2. Enter bid amount (e.g., 1000 RFRM)
3. Approve token lock transaction
4. RFRM escrowed in auction contract
5. Become highest bidder
6. Wait for auction end
```

**Bidding Rules:**
- Must have RFRM tokens in wallet
- Previous bidder's tokens unlocked when outbid
- Winner's tokens locked for entire market duration
- Tokens returned when market closes

### Phase 3: NFT Minting & Management

Winner receives:
1. **Gookie NFT** minted to their wallet
2. **Market management dashboard** access
3. **30% yield share** from market (in RFRM)
4. **Public badge** showing their Gookie ownership

## Penalties & Risk

### Early Exit Penalty

If you abandon your Gookie before market closes:

```
Penalty = Locked RFRM × (Time Remaining / Total Duration) × 25%
```

**Example:**
- RFRM locked: 1000 tokens
- Total duration: 60 days
- Time elapsed: 20 days
- Time remaining: 40 days
- **Penalty: 1000 × (40/60) × 0.25 = 166.67 RFRM seized**

- ❌ Engaging in fraud or scams

## FAQ

### Can I sell my Gookie NFT?
No, Gookies are **non-transferable** during the rental period. After market closes, the NFT becomes a collectible but loses management rights.

### What if my market fails to attract participants?
You still earn yield proportional to whatever SOL is locked. Even small markets generate some earnings.

### Can I manage multiple Gookies?
Yes! You can bid on and manage multiple Gookies simultaneously. Volume-based discounts apply.

### What if I lose my private key?
Your locked RFRM and NFT are permanently lost. **Always secure your seed phrase.**
